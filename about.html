// About Shridhuu

Fansubbing & Donghua.

Hey, it's me, Shridhuu.

I'm 18.5 (almost 19) years old, and my birthday is on May 16.

I pair manual translation with AI-assisted drafting, cross-checking source novels and wikis so cultivation realms, sects, and character names stay accurate. (Sometimes I'm just lazy.)

All my subtitles are shared free for the community through Donghua Central on Discord.

Release Cadence

Most episodes go up on the same day the raws drop (typically within 3-6 hours). School or exams can occasionally push a release back by 1-3 days.

Check the weekly schedule for live local-time countdowns, or grab release ping roles in Discord to get notified the moment new subs go live.
